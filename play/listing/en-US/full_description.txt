Talk to Claude Code on the way to work. Dictate a task when you set off, hear what Claude did when it's done, and say yes or no before anything risky runs, all without taking your eyes off the road.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick a conversation in Android Auto, tap the mic and say what you want done.
• Claude Code works on your own computer, on this phone, or in the cloud, and its replies are read aloud.
• When Claude wants to run a command or change files, you're asked first. Say "yes" or "no".
• Replies also arrive as Android Auto message notifications, so you can answer by voice from any screen.

WHERE CLAUDE RUNS (YOU CHOOSE)
• My computer or Raspberry Pi: install a small, open-source bridge with one command and scan its QR code. Claude Code uses your repos and your own Claude login. The connection is encrypted and pinned to your computer.
• This phone: runs Claude Code in Termux on the same phone, no computer needed.
• Cloud: Anthropic-hosted sandboxes with your GitHub repos, using your own Anthropic API key (billed by Anthropic per use).

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
There is no Drive Mode account and no server of ours. Your messages go only to the place you pick: your computer, this phone, or Anthropic's API. Speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognition service. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

WHAT YOU NEED
• Android Auto (phone or car display) for the car experience. The app also works on the phone by itself.
• A Claude subscription or an Anthropic API key, depending on where Claude runs.
